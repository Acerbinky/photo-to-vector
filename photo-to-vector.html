Photo → Vector

Take a picture, trace it into clean vector paths, and download a real SVG — ready for a laser, Cricut, patch, sticker, or logo. Everything happens on your phone, nothing is uploaded.

1Get a photo

Live camera works in most browsers. If your browser blocks it inside this app, “Take photo” opens your phone’s native camera instead — same result.

Ready. For the cleanest trace, shoot a subject with strong contrast — a logo, sign, silhouette, or black-and-white art works best. Busy photos work too in Poster mode.
No photo handy? Test the tracer instantly.
Tips for laser-ready results
  • Use B&W Silhouette mode, raise Detail, and adjust Threshold until the shape is solid black with clean edges.
  • Fill light gaps before you shoot — shadows become extra paths.
  • Downloaded SVG uses real paths (not an embedded photo), so it scales and cuts cleanly.
  • For a patch or sticker, Poster mode with 3–5 colors keeps the file simple.

2Trace it

Change a setting and the vector updates. Start with a preset, then fine-tune.

Add a photo — your vector will appear in the “Your vector” section below.
